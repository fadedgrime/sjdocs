# Ranking Guide

:::note DISCLAIMER

This guide is intended for **Corporate Officer** and **higher ranks**. Its purpose is to provide clear instructions for promoting or demoting players.

:::

# Information
When ranking, promoting, or demoting staff members, we enforce strict guidelines to prevent misuse or abuse of authority. Ranking is conducted through the **Neptune** bot within our Discord server. Only **Corporate Officers** and above have permission to rank players.

When performing any ranking actions, ensure they are properly logged. **Promotions or demotions that lack reasonable grounds are strictly prohibited.**

## Cooldowns
To ensure **fairness** and **prevent staff from receiving multiple promotions in a single day**, we have implemented cooldown periods. These cooldowns provide everyone an equal opportunity to earn a promotion. The table below outlines the ranks subject to cooldowns and their respective durations:

| Rank Group | Cooldown Period |
|:----------------|:------------------|
| Junior Team Member, Senior Team Member, Floor Monitor, Security Officer (LR) | No cooldown period |
| Operations Technician, Operations Supervisor, Assistant Floor Manager, Floor Manager (MR) | 1-week cooldown period |
| Corporate Officer, Executive Officer, Board of Directors (HR) | 2-week cooldown period |

> During Mass Shifts, staff members are eligible for a promotion even if they are within their cooldown period. **Vice Presidents** and **above** are authorized to host mass shifts, while **Junior Team Members** and **above** may participate. To qualify for a promotion during a mass shift, staff members must **arrive on time** and **remain until the shift concludes**. Leaving early **will disqualify** them from receiving a promotion.

## Promotions
When promoting a staff member, ensure they are not on a cooldown period and are not an **Floor Manager** or above. SHR+ will specifically look for Floor Managers to promote. **Corporate Officers and Executive Officers** are prohibited from promoting Floor Managers to Corporate Officer.

To promote someone, use the following command in our Discord server: ``/promote (Roblox username)``

**Do not include parentheses () around the username. Be sure to enter the correct and full Roblox username, as any errors will prevent the promotion**.

:::danger PROMOTED THE WRONG PERSON?

If you accidentally promote the wrong person, please demote them and ensure the intended staff member receives their promotion.

:::

## Demotions
When demoting a staff member, ensure the demotion is reasonable. **Floor Managers** and **above** may request a demotion by providing the following:
* **A clear explanation of the staff member’s actions**
* **Evidence supporting the claim**
* **The offender’s Roblox username and Discord username** (if applicable)

Only **SHR** and **above** are authorized to carry out staff demotions. Staff members should not be demoted for the following reasons:
* **Trolling** (Issue a warning and then have an HR+ server ban. If continued, a demotion is applicable.)
* **Being in a blacklisted group**
* **Exploiting** (Permanent ban if under LR. Permanent ban & demotion for MR+.)
* **Other unreasonable reasons that do not align with our guidelines**

To demote someone, use the following command in our Discord server: ``/demote (Roblox username)``

All demotions must be logged. Failure to log a demotion will result in consequences. Use the **#demotion-request** channel to submit demotion requests.

**Do not include parentheses () around the username. Be sure to enter the correct and full Roblox username, as any errors will prevent the demotion**.

:::danger DEMOTED THE WRONG PERSON?

If you mistakenly demote the wrong person, restore their rank and ensure the intended staff member receives the demotion.

:::
